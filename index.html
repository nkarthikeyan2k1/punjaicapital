About us

A community built on early mornings and village roads.

We're a community club from Punjai Puliampatti for running, cycling and endurance sports. Beginners, comebacks and seasoned racers train side by side, and nobody gets left at the back.

Read our story

Punjai Capitals Sports Club is a community-driven sports club based in the Erode district of Tamil Nadu, bringing together passionate individuals who share a love for sports, fitness, and an active lifestyle. Our club focuses on running, cycling, and endurance sports, encouraging athletes of all experience levels to challenge themselves, improve their performance, and achieve their personal goals.

We believe in building a strong sporting community through teamwork, discipline, consistency, and mutual support. From group runs and cycling sessions to fitness awareness and knowledge sharing, we aim to create an environment where every individual can learn, grow, and perform better.

“Grounded by Punjai. Driven by pace.”

Our vision

To inspire more people to embrace sports, build healthier lifestyles, and grow together as a strong and united sporting community.

Our motto

Together we train. Together we grow. Together we achieve.

What we do

Run. Ride. Swim.

One team, multiple sports. Every session is open to beginners.

Run

Group runs at every pace, from your first 2K to long runs before race day. Walk breaks are totally fine.

Ride

Group rides on the roads around Punjai. Steady pace, a safe group, and we regroup so nobody rides alone.

Swim

Swimming for fitness and cross-training, and a first step towards triathlon.

Endure

Training for half marathons, marathons and beyond, with people who'll keep you company on the long ones.

First-timer guide

New to running? Start here.

Here's what people usually ask before their first club run.

Still unsure? Message us on Instagram

I've never run before. Can I join?+

Yes, that's exactly who we're for. Start with walk–run, go at your own pace, and the group waits for you at the end.

What pace do I need?+

Any pace. There's no cut-off time and nobody gets left behind.

What should I bring?+

Comfortable shoes, a water bottle and light clothes. A cap helps once the sun is up.

Is it free?+

Yes. Membership is completely free. No fees, no catch.

Who leads the sessions?+

Our coaches and captains plan each session and stay with the group. You'll meet them at your first run.

How do I join?+
